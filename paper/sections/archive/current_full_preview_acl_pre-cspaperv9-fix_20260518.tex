\title{EvidenceFlow: Query-Conditioned Evidence Propagation for Multi-Hop Retrieval}
\author{Anonymous ACL submission}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Multi-hop question answering requires retrieval systems to surface
evidence that is not only individually relevant to a question, but also
connected across documents and covering the entities and relations needed
by a downstream reader. We present \methodname{}, a graph-based retrieval
framework that models multi-hop retrieval as query-conditioned evidence
propagation over a document-grounded OpenIE graph. \methodname{} builds a
fact-source substrate that promotes each OpenIE fact to a graph node and
binds it to its source passage, runs query-local personalized PageRank
over induced fact nodes with passage provenance to obtain an evidence
flow, and refines the final ranked list through relation-grounded
expansion and coverage-aware reranking. Experiments on HotpotQA,
2WikiMultiHopQA, and MuSiQue compare \methodname{} against
no-retrieval, sparse and dense, hierarchical tree, OpenIE-based graph,
and path/chain-oriented baselines under a unified GPT-4o-mini reader.
The results show consistent gains in
retrieval quality and downstream QA, with ablations isolating the
contribution of local evidence propagation, evidence-need mining,
and coverage-aware reranking.
\end{abstract}
