\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Standard RAG.}
Standard RAG ranks passages by lexical or dense similarity to the
question, with BM25 \citep{robertson2009bm25} and
DPR \citep{karpukhin2020dpr} as canonical baselines and
RAPTOR \citep{sarthi2024raptor} adding a hierarchical retrieval
tree. Iterative chain-of-thought retrievers such as
IRCoT \citep{trivedi2023ircot} and Self-Ask
\citep{press2023selfask} drive multi-hop retrieval through generated
reasoning steps. \emph{Limitation.} The retrieval state stays a
list or beam of passages without explicit cross-document structure,
so bridge passages with low surface similarity are systematically
underweighted.

\paragraph{Graph RAG.}
A second line organizes the corpus into an explicit graph.
HippoRAG~2 \citep{gutierrez2025hipporag} runs personalized PageRank
over a passage--phrase graph, PropRAG \citep{wang2025proprag} beam-
searches over a proposition graph, GraphRAG
\citep{edge2024graphrag} aggregates community summaries, and
HGRAG \citep{hgrag2024} propagates over an entity-passage hypergraph;
recent variants explore multi-granular
\citep{hu2026mgrangrag}, sentence-level \citep{liang2026sentgraph},
and CPU-only \citep{wang2026sprig} extensions, while earlier graph
readers integrate evidence via message
passing \citep{qiu2019dfgn,song2022evidenceintegration}.
\emph{Limitation.} These systems condition on a pre-built global
structure, so question conditioning enters only through seeds or
post-hoc search; node-level relevance does not directly produce a
short reader-facing list that is both connected and covering.

\paragraph{Evidence-enhanced RAG.}
A third line refines the evidence reaching the reader.
NeocorRAG \citep{peng2026neocorrag} mines evidence chains from
retrieved text and uses them to filter passages; Relink
\citep{huang2026relink} reconstructs query-specific evidence graphs
to repair KG incompleteness; ToG \citep{sun2024tog} and
KAPING \citep{baek2023kaping} traverse or retrieve subgraphs from a
pre-built KG. \emph{Limitation.} These methods operate \emph{on top
of} an underlying retriever or KG, so they inherit upstream misses,
depend on KG completeness, and incur per-question LLM-guided
traversal cost.

\paragraph{Summary.}
None of the three lines treats the conversion of graph relevance
into a connected and covering reader-facing passage list as its
central object. \methodname{} addresses this conversion by
combining query-local propagation over a fact-source substrate
with evidence-need mining and coverage-aware reranking.
